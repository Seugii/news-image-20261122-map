Entry for the [make.fun bounty **news-image-20261122**](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261122)
— "Sum up 22 November's world news in one image".

A world map is the central visual: every story is pinned to the place it
happened, and the date — **Sunday 22 November 2026** — is the huge headline.
13 stories across 6 regions, numbered on the map and listed below it, plus a
Bitcoin price chart (18–22 Nov) and a red-bordered DATE CHECK box.

## How it is built

`entry.png` (1024×1024, deterministic) is pre-rendered. `main.py` is
stdlib-only: it copies `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`.
